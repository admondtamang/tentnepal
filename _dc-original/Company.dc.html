The Company

Catering to the tourism trade — and to relief when crisis strikes.

About us

Nepal Tent & Tarpaulin P. Ltd. was established in 1980 to fill a gap in the supply of trekking and camping products to the tourism industry.

The 1970s and 80s were a period when Nepal faced an upsurge in tourism and travel. Demand for trekkers’ tents, nylon carryalls, over-nighters, garden umbrellas and similar products was growing by the day — yet there was hardly any manufacturing unit able to cope.

Nepal Tent & Tarpaulin was established to fill that vacuum. Over the decades we have aspired for excellence, and are now recognised as a manufacturer and exporter of quality products to many parts of the world.

We rightfully claim to have contributed our own bit to the expansion of the tourism industry in our country — and we continue to actively serve demands all around the globe.
